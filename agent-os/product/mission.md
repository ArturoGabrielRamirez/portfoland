# Product Mission

## Pitch

Portfoland is a gamified career storytelling platform that helps job seekers, students, and professionals create compelling portfolios and CVs by providing an interactive pixelated timeline experience, AI-powered content generation, and personalized subdomain hosting.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Job Seekers**: Individuals actively looking for employment who need professional CVs and portfolios but lack the technical skills or knowledge to create them
- **Students & Recent Graduates**: People transitioning from education to career who need to articulate their journey and skills effectively
- **Career Changers**: Professionals pivoting to new fields who need to present transferable skills in a compelling way
- **Recruiters**: HR professionals and hiring managers viewing candidate portfolios and timelines

### User Personas

**Maria** (22-28)
- **Role:** Recent university graduate / entry-level job seeker
- **Context:** Has completed education and possibly internships, but struggles to present her experience professionally
- **Pain Points:** Does not know what to include in a CV, unsure how to express soft skills, no technical ability to create a portfolio website
- **Goals:** Land her first professional job with a standout application that tells her story

**Carlos** (30-40)
- **Role:** Mid-career professional / career changer
- **Context:** Has years of experience but is pivoting to a new industry or role
- **Pain Points:** Difficulty translating past experience to new field, outdated CV format, wants to show growth trajectory
- **Goals:** Present career journey in a way that highlights transferable skills and continuous learning

**Lucia** (18-25)
- **Role:** Student / self-taught learner
- **Context:** Building skills through courses, bootcamps, and self-study while still in school or recently graduated
- **Pain Points:** No formal work experience to show, struggles to articulate project-based learning
- **Goals:** Create a professional presence that showcases skills and learning journey despite limited traditional experience

**Roberto** (35-50)
- **Role:** Recruiter / HR professional
- **Context:** Reviews hundreds of CVs and portfolios weekly, looking for standout candidates
- **Pain Points:** Generic CVs that all look the same, difficulty understanding candidate journeys quickly
- **Goals:** Efficiently identify promising candidates through engaging, informative presentations

## The Problem

### Career Storytelling is Hard

Most people struggle to articulate their professional journey effectively. Traditional CVs are static documents that fail to capture growth, learning paths, and the interconnected nature of skills and experiences. This results in missed opportunities for both job seekers and employers.

**Our Solution:** Transform career storytelling into an interactive, gamified experience where users map their journey on a pixelated timeline, visualize their skill progression like a video game skill tree, and receive AI-powered guidance to express what they often cannot articulate themselves.

### Technical Barriers to Professional Presentation

Creating a professional portfolio website requires technical skills that many job seekers do not possess. Existing solutions are either too complex, too expensive, or produce generic-looking results.

**Our Solution:** Provide a platform where anyone can create a unique, visually engaging portfolio with their own subdomain (username.portfoland.com), using playful themes (retro, comic, pixel art) that stand out from traditional portfolios.

### CV Creation Knowledge Gap

Many people, especially those early in their careers, do not know what to include in a CV, how to structure it, or how to present their experiences in the best light.

**Our Solution:** An AI assistant that asks guiding questions, helps users understand what matters in their field, and generates professional CVs tailored to their career path and target industry.

## Differentiators

### Gamified Career Visualization

Unlike traditional portfolio builders that present static information, Portfoland transforms career journeys into interactive pixelated timelines with customizable characters, geographic mapping, and video game-style skill trees. This creates memorable, engaging presentations that stand out to recruiters.

### Field-Specific Intelligence

Unlike generic CV tools, Portfoland adapts dynamically to different career fields. A programmer sees technologies and languages in their skill tree, while a lawyer sees courses, certifications, and specializations. This contextual awareness ensures relevant, industry-appropriate presentations.

### AI-Powered Storytelling Assistance

Unlike tools that simply format information, our AI assistant actively helps users discover and articulate their value. Through guided questions, it helps users recognize achievements they might overlook and express skills they struggle to describe.

### Shareable Achievement Visualization

At the end of the timeline creation process, users receive a generated image featuring their character alongside all achievements - perfect for sharing on social media and creating buzz around their professional journey.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Interactive Pixelated Timeline:** Map your career journey on a stylized map of Argentina (expandable to other regions) with selectable characters representing different ethnicities and fun options (wizard, troll, etc.)
- **Video Game Skill Tree:** Visualize skill progression in an engaging, gamified format that adapts to your career field
- **Personal Portfolio Generator:** Create professional portfolios hosted on personalized subdomains (username.portfoland.com)
- **AI CV Generator:** Generate professional, tailored CVs with AI assistance using Vercel AI SDK

### Collaboration Features

- **Social Sharing:** Share timelines, achievements, and portfolios across social platforms
- **Recruiter View Mode:** Optimized viewing experience for recruiters reviewing candidate profiles

### Advanced Features

- **AI Story Assistant:** Guided conversations that help users identify and articulate their professional value
- **Multi-language Support:** Full English and Spanish localization with next-intl
- **Achievement Image Generator:** Create shareable images featuring your character and accomplishments
- **Field-Dynamic Content:** Automatically adapt skill trees and prompts based on career field (tech, law, design, etc.)
